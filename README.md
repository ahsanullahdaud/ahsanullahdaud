At **EZ Consultants & ERP Solutions** I work on the full lifecycle of features for our ERP product:

- Writing and tuning **T-SQL** and stored procedures for finance reports and month-end processes
- Deploying releases to client servers and supporting them in production, including out-of-hours fixes
